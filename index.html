FAQ

Good questions

Do you really come to me?

Yes — RealShine is 100% mobile. We valet your car at home, at work or wherever it's parked across Louth, Cavan, Meath & Monaghan.

How long does a valet take?

A mini valet on a car is around 1–1.5 hours. A full Platinum valet takes up to 3 hours on a car and up to 3.5 hours on an SUV or 7-seater.

How much does it cost?

Vital from €110, Silver from €135 and Platinum from €225 for a normal car. SUVs, Jeeps and 7-seaters cost a little more. Heavily soiled vehicles may need a quote.

Can I get a custom package?

Absolutely. Just want seats shampooed or pet hair gone? Tell us what you need on WhatsApp and we'll price it.

What's the difference between detailing and a car wash?

A car wash cleans the surface. Detailing goes deeper — steam, shampoo, extraction, trims, tyres and the spots a wash never reaches — to bring the whole car back to its best.

Are your products eco-friendly?

Yes. We use eco-friendly products, ECO-steaming (less water, fewer chemicals) and energy-efficient equipment.
